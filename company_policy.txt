At TechStream, we are committed to providing the best electronics to our customers.

RETURN POLICY:
We offer a 14-day return policy for all electronics, starting from the date of delivery.
However, there is a special exception for laptops: a laptop must be returned unopened with the original factory seal intact.
If the seal on a laptop box is broken, we cannot accept the return due to security and data privacy reasons.

SHIPPING INFO:
Shipping is free for all orders over $50 within the continental United States.
For orders under $50, a flat shipping fee of $5.99 applies.
International shipping is currently not available.

SUPPORT HOURS:
Our technical support team is available Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
We are closed on weekends and holidays.
